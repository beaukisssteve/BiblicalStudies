# Sabbath

**First appearance of seventh-day rest:** [[Genesis 01]]; Genesis 2:1–3.

## Hebrew
*Shavat* (cease); *Shabbat* (Sabbath).

## Biblical text
Genesis 2:1–3; Exodus 20:8–11; Deuteronomy 5:12–15; Hebrews 4:1–11.

## Interpretation
Different Christian traditions interpret the continuing application of the Sabbath command differently.
