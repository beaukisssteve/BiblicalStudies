# 📖 **Leviticus Survey**

### **Leviticus**

- **Hebrew Name:** _Vayikra_ (וַיִּקְרָא — “And He called”)
- **Meaning:** “And He called…” (God calling Moses from the tabernacle)
- **Author:** Moses
- **Date Written:** ~1445–1405 BC
- **Time Period Covered:** ~1 month at Mount Sinai

---

## **Chapter Summary**

1. Burnt offering (total consecration).
2. Grain offering (thanksgiving and devotion).
3. Peace offering (fellowship with God).
4. Sin offering (atonement for unintentional sin).
5. Guilt offering (restitution and forgiveness).
6. Additional instructions for offerings.
7. Laws governing priestly portions of sacrifices.
8. Ordination of Aaron and his sons.
9. Priests begin ministry; God’s glory appears.
10. Nadab and Abihu judged for unauthorized fire.
11. Clean and unclean animals defined.
12. Purification after childbirth.
13. Laws concerning skin diseases (leprosy).
14. Cleansing rituals for disease and houses.
15. Bodily discharges and impurity laws.
16. Day of Atonement (Yom Kippur).
17. Regulations about blood and sacrifice location.
18. Laws on sexual purity.
19. Holiness in daily life (“Be holy…”).
20. Penalties for sin and idolatry.
21. Holiness required of priests.
22. Acceptable offerings and priestly conduct.
23. Appointed feasts of the Lord.
24. Lampstand, bread, and justice laws.
25. Sabbath year and Year of Jubilee.
26. Blessings for obedience, curses for disobedience.
27. Laws concerning vows and dedications.

---

## **Covenants**

- **Mosaic Covenant (Expanded) — Leviticus 1–27**
    
    - Detailed stipulations for holiness, worship, and sacrifice
    - Defines how Israel lives as God’s covenant people
- **Covenant Blessings and Curses — Leviticus 26**
    
    - Obedience → blessing
    - Disobedience → judgment and exile
    - Yet includes promise of restoration (26:40–45)

---

## **God’s Character Revealed**

- **Holy** — Central theme (“Be holy, for I am holy” — 19:2)
- **Just** — Sin requires punishment (10; 20)
- **Merciful** — Provides sacrificial system for atonement
- **Gracious** — Accepts substitutionary offerings
- **Precise / Orderly** — Worship must be done His way
- **Present** — Dwells among His people in the tabernacle
- **Righteous** — Defines moral, ceremonial, and social order
- **Jealous for Worship** — Judges improper worship (Nadab & Abihu)

---

## **Major Themes**

- Holiness (the dominant theme)
- Atonement through sacrifice
- Substitution (life for life)
- Cleansing from impurity
- Worship according to God’s design
- Priesthood and mediation
- Separation from sin and the nations
- Blood as the means of atonement
- God dwelling among His people

---

## **Key Redemptive Texts**

- **Leviticus 16** — Day of Atonement (central redemptive chapter)
- **Leviticus 17:11** — “The life is in the blood… it makes atonement”
- **Leviticus 19:2** — Call to holiness
- **Leviticus 26:40–45** — Promise of covenant restoration

---

## **Cross-References to NT Fulfillment**

- **Sacrificial System (Leviticus 1–7)**  
    → Hebrews 10:1–14 (Christ fulfills all sacrifices)
    
- **Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16)**  
    → Hebrews 9–10 (Christ as the final High Priest and sacrifice)
    
- **Scapegoat (Leviticus 16:21–22)**  
    → 2 Corinthians 5:21 (Christ bears our sin)
    
- **Blood Atonement (Leviticus 17:11)**  
    → Hebrews 9:22 (“Without shedding of blood…”)
    
- **Priesthood (Leviticus 8–9)**  
    → Hebrews 7 (Christ as eternal High Priest)
    
- **Holiness Command (Leviticus 19:2)**  
    → 1 Peter 1:15–16
    
- **Clean/Unclean Laws (Leviticus 11)**  
    → Mark 7:18–19 (fulfilled in Christ)
    

---

## **Typology (This is the engine room of Leviticus)**

- **High Priest → Christ**
    
    - Mediator between God and man
    - Enters presence of God on behalf of people
- **Sacrifices → Christ**
    
    - Burnt offering → total surrender
    - Sin offering → atonement
    - Guilt offering → restitution
    - Peace offering → fellowship
- **Scapegoat → Christ**
    
    - Sin carried away from the people
- **Tabernacle System → Christ**
    
    - Access to God through mediation
- **Blood → Christ’s Blood**
    
    - Required for forgiveness

---

## Straight Talk

Leviticus answers a brutal but necessary question:

👉 _How does a holy God not destroy sinful people living in His presence?_

Answer:

- Blood
- Substitution
- Mediation
- Obedience

Or to say it plainly:

**Leviticus is the theology of the cross… before the cross shows up.**

---

## What Most People Miss

People read Leviticus and think:

What it actually is:

> **A detailed blueprint explaining why Jesus had to die the way He did.**

You don’t understand:

- Hebrews
- The priesthood of Christ
- Atonement

…unless you understand this book.
