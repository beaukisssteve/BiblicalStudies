# 📖 **Exodus Survey**

### **Exodus**

- **Hebrew Name:** _Shemot_ (שְׁמוֹת — “Names”)
- **Meaning:** “These are the names…” (opening words of the book)
- **Author:** Moses
- **Date Written:** ~1445–1405 BC
- **Time Period Covered:** ~400 years (Israel in Egypt to Sinai)

---

## **Chapter Summary**

1. Israel multiplies in Egypt; oppression begins.
2. Birth and preservation of Moses.
3. God calls Moses from the burning bush.
4. Moses returns to Egypt with signs from God.
5. Pharaoh increases Israel’s burden.
6. God reaffirms His covenant with Abraham.
7. Moses confronts Pharaoh; plagues begin.
8. Plagues continue (frogs, gnats, flies).
9. More plagues (livestock, boils, hail).
10. Locusts and darkness plague Egypt.
11. Death of the firstborn announced.
12. Passover instituted; firstborn struck; Exodus begins.
13. Consecration of firstborn; God leads by pillar.
14. Red Sea crossing; Egypt destroyed.
15. Song of Moses; bitter water made sweet.
16. Manna and quail provided.
17. Water from the rock; battle with Amalek.
18. Jethro advises Moses on leadership.
19. Israel arrives at Sinai; covenant prepared.
20. Ten Commandments given.
21. Laws concerning servants and violence.
22. Laws on property, justice, and morality.
23. Further laws; promise of conquest.
24. Covenant confirmed with blood.
25. Instructions for the Ark and Tabernacle.
26. Tabernacle structure described.
27. Altar and courtyard instructions.
28. Priestly garments designed.
29. Priests consecrated.
30. Altar of incense; atonement money.
31. Bezalel appointed; Sabbath emphasized.
32. Golden calf; Israel sins.
33. God’s presence threatened; Moses intercedes.
34. Covenant renewed; God reveals His name.
35. Offerings for the Tabernacle.
36. Tabernacle construction begins.
37. Ark, table, lampstand made.
38. Altar and courtyard completed.
39. Priestly garments completed.
40. Tabernacle erected; God’s glory fills it.

---

## **Covenants**

- **Abrahamic Covenant (Reaffirmed) — Exodus 2:24; 6:5–8**
    
    - God remembers His promise to Abraham, Isaac, Jacob
    - Fulfilled in deliverance from Egypt
- **Mosaic Covenant — Exodus 19–24**
    
    - Conditional covenant (“If you obey…”)
    - Law given (Ten Commandments + case laws)
    - Israel becomes a “kingdom of priests”
- **Covenant Renewal — Exodus 34**
    
    - After the golden calf rebellion
    - Demonstrates both justice and mercy

---

## **God’s Character Revealed**

- **Redeemer** — Delivers Israel from slavery (6:6)
- **All-Powerful (Sovereign)** — Over Pharaoh and creation (plagues)
- **Holy** — Sinai trembles; boundaries set (19–20)
- **Just** — Judges Egypt and Israel’s sin
- **Merciful / Compassionate** — Forgives after golden calf (34:6–7)
- **Faithful** — Remembers covenant promises
- **Provider** — Manna, water, guidance
- **Present** — Pillar of cloud/fire; tabernacle glory
- **Jealous for Worship** — Rejects idolatry (32)

---

## **Major Themes**

- Redemption from bondage
- God vs false gods (Egypt’s plagues)
- Covenant relationship
- Law and holiness
- Worship (Tabernacle system)
- Mediation (Moses as mediator)
- God dwelling with His people
- Obedience vs rebellion
- Blood atonement

---

## **Key Redemptive Texts**

- **Exodus 6:6–7** — “I will redeem you…”
- **Exodus 12:13** — Blood of the lamb saves from judgment
- **Exodus 19:5–6** — Kingdom of priests
- **Exodus 24:8** — Covenant sealed with blood
- **Exodus 34:6–7** — God’s self-revelation of mercy and justice

## **Cross-References to NT Fulfillment**

- **Passover Lamb (Exodus 12)**  
    → John 1:29 (“Lamb of God”)  
    → 1 Corinthians 5:7 (Christ our Passover)
    
- **Exodus (Deliverance)**  
    → Luke 9:31 (Jesus’ “exodus” in Greek)
    
- **Manna (Exodus 16)**  
    → John 6:32–35 (Jesus the true bread)
    
- **Water from Rock (Exodus 17)**  
    → 1 Corinthians 10:4 (Christ the rock)
    
- **Law (Exodus 20)**  
    → Matthew 5 (Christ fulfills the law)
    
- **Covenant Blood (Exodus 24:8)**  
    → Luke 22:20 (New Covenant in Christ’s blood)
    
- **Tabernacle (Exodus 25–40)**  
    → John 1:14 (“dwelt/tabernacled among us”)  
    → Hebrews 8–10 (heavenly fulfillment)

## Straight Talk

Genesis says: _“God will save.”_  
Exodus says: _“Here’s what that looks like.”_

You’ve got:

- Blood substitution
- Deliverance from slavery
- Covenant law
- God dwelling with His people

That’s basically the entire New Testament in seed form.
